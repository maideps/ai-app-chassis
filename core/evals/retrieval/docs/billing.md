# Billing and invoices

Plans are billed monthly or yearly in advance. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and sent to the billing e-mail address; past invoices can be downloaded as PDF from Settings, Billing.

Adding seats in the middle of a period is charged pro rata on the next invoice. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal; there are no refunds for unused seats.

To change the company name, VAT number or address printed on invoices, edit the billing profile before the next invoice is issued. Invoices already issued cannot be changed.
